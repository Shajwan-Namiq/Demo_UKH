Comprehensive Support

Student Services & Support

From your first day of orientation to graduation day, dedicated professional offices ensure your wellness, career readiness, and academic success.

  • Career Development Center: Resume reviews, interview clinics, internship placements, and alumni networking.
  • International Student Advisory: Full visa assistance, residency paperwork, and airport greeting for non-resident scholars.
  • Student Counseling & Health Clinic: Confidential mental wellness support, peer listening pods, and primary medical triage.
  • Academic Writing & Tutoring Pods: One-on-one English academic writing coaching and quantitative science tutoring.
🏡

On-Campus Student Housing

UKH provides secure, fully furnished student residences within walking distance of academic lecture theatres, dining halls, and libraries in Erbil.

  • Single and double en-suite rooms with study desks and high-speed fiber internet
  • Communal student kitchens, study lounges, and modern laundry facilities
  • 24/7 building security, biometric access, and resident assistant mentorship
