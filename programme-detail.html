Programme Overview

The Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence at UKH prepares forward-thinking technologists and researchers to pioneer next-generation computational systems. Students gain rigorous theoretical foundations and hands-on laboratory mastery in machine learning algorithms, natural language processing, neural computing, robotics, and cloud-scale AI architectures.

Key Highlights & Research Facilities

  • Access to the UKH High-Performance GPU Cluster & Robotics Simulation Lab
  • Direct collaboration with global tech innovators and research consortia
  • Opportunity for joint thesis projects with partner European universities
  • Capstone dissertation solving urgent regional and industrial challenges

Curriculum & Modules

The curriculum balances mathematical rigour with practical software craftsmanship, divided across two academic terms and a dedicated summer research dissertation.

Term 1 • Core

Deep Learning & Neural Architectures

Backpropagation, CNNs, transformers, attention mechanisms, and multi-GPU PyTorch model training.

Term 1 • Core

Advanced Machine Learning & Stats

Probabilistic graphical models, Bayesian inference, supervised/unsupervised algorithms, and dimensionality reduction.

Term 2 • Core

Computer Vision & Pattern Recognition

Feature extraction, object detection, 3D vision, generative adversarial models, and medical image diagnostics.

Term 2 • Core

Natural Language Processing & LLMs

Semantic analysis, tokenization, transformer LLM fine-tuning, and multilingual translation for Kurdish dialects.

Term 2 • Elective

Autonomous Robotics & Control

Kinematics, ROS 2 pipelines, path planning, reinforcement learning, and sensor fusion for mobile rovers.

Summer Term • Capstone

Master's Research Dissertation

Original 15,000-word empirical research paper or applied industrial software thesis defended before an academic panel.

Entry Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree (BSc) in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Mathematics, or closely related STEM discipline with minimum 65% aggregate score
  • English Language Proficiency: Official IELTS Academic 6.0 (or TOEFL iBT 75+, or passing grade on the UKH English Language Placement Exam)
  • A comprehensive Statement of Purpose detailing proposed research interest in AI / Machine Learning
  • Academic interview with the School of Science & Engineering Admissions Panel

Career Opportunities

Graduates of the MSc in Artificial Intelligence enter senior engineering, data science, and academic research positions globally:

💼 Machine Learning Engineer 🔬 AI Research Scientist 📊 Lead Data Scientist 🤖 Autonomous Systems Architect 🏛️ University Lecturer / Doctoral Candidate
