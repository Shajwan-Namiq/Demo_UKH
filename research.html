Domains of Inquiry

Research Areas

Our interdisciplinary research clusters address foundational challenges facing Kurdistan and the global community.

🤖

Artificial Intelligence

Foundation models, natural language processing for low-resource Kurdish dialects, computer vision, autonomous rovers, and ethical algorithm governance.

🩺

Health & Medicine

Emergency trauma resuscitation protocols, high-fidelity clinical simulation, epidemiological mapping, and AI diagnostics for oncology.

⚙️

Engineering

Earthquake-resilient structural mechanics, smart microgrids, geotechnical foundations, and DevOps pipelines for cloud software.

⚖️

Social Sciences

Middle East geopolitics, international diplomatic treaties, constitutional governance, post-conflict economic diversification, and gender equity.

🌿

Environment & Water

Groundwater hydrology in the Erbil basin, climate adaptation, solar renewable transitions, and sustainable agricultural technologies.

🕊️

Peace & Dialogue

Centre for Regional Policy think tank hosting diplomatic negotiations, cross-cultural youth exchanges, and public policy working papers.

Multi-Year Research Initiative
Digital Sovereignty & NLP

AI for Kurdish Language Technologies

Developing the largest open-access Kurdish linguistic corpus, pre-trained transformer foundation models (KurdoLLM), and speech-to-text engines for Sorani and Kurmanji. Conducted in partnership with European academic consortia.

Institutes & Facilities

Research Centres

Specialized university laboratories and think tanks leading cutting-edge inquiry in Erbil.

🤖

Advanced AI & Robotics Laboratory

Equipped with multi-GPU high-performance clusters, robot manipulator arms, and high-speed vision capture systems. Incubating enterprise AI startups and doctoral engineering research.

🏛️

Centre for Regional Policy & Peace Studies

An independent academic think tank bridging government ministers, international ambassadors, and scholars to formulate policy strategies for economic reform and regional stability.

🩺

Clinical Simulation & Health Research Unit

WFME-recognized medical simulation complex featuring computerized anatomical tables, interactive patient manikins, and surgical simulation suites for clinicians across Iraq.

🌿

Sustainable Infrastructure & Water Institute

Conducting hydro-geological soil testing, concrete resilience analysis, and solar energy integration models for urban municipal planners throughout the Kurdistan Region.
