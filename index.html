WHY UKH?

Founded in 2006, the University of Kurdistan Hewlêr is the premier English-medium public institution in the Kurdistan Region, dedicated to fostering visionary leadership, scientific inquiry, and global impact.

🏛️

Academic Excellence

Rigorous British and international higher education frameworks with 100% English medium instruction across all degree levels.

  • Faculty from top global universities
  • UK QAA aligned curriculum quality

Research & Innovation
