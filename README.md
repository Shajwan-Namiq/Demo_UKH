# Demo_UKH

> **University of Kurdistan Hewlêr (UKH) — Modern AI-Integrated Web Platform Prototype**  
> Erbil, Kurdistan Region of Iraq | Aligned with British Quality Assurance Frameworks

A prestigious, modern university web prototype for the **University of Kurdistan Hewlêr (UKH)**, inspired by the academic authority and visual elegance of Oxford, Cambridge, and international top-tier universities.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🤖 UKH AI Academic Navigator & Assistant
- **Hero AI Academic Discovery Console**: Embedded search and prompt chips directly in the hero banner for immediate admissions, degrees, and scholarship answers.
- **Floating UKH AI Assistant**: Natural language university intelligence engine answering queries about programmes, entry requirements, fees, housing, and campus life.
- **AI Programme Finder**: Multi-attribute filtering (interest and degree level) with calculated % match scoring and real-time curriculum recommendation.
- **"Your UKH Journey" AI Adaptive Experience**: Role-based pathways customized for prospective students, current students, researchers, faculty, and alumni.
- **AI Admissions Guide Wizard**: Step-by-step interactive degree evaluator checking entry criteria and fast-track eligibility.

### 2. 🏛️ Four World-Class Academic Schools Showcase
Showcasing UKH's core academic faculties:
- **School of Science & Engineering** (MSc Artificial Intelligence, BSc Computer Science, Software Engineering, Civil Infrastructure)
- **School of Medicine** (British-curriculum MBChB, MSc Public Health, Clinical Simulation Center)
- **School of Management & Economics** (Executive MBA, BSc Business Administration, Finance & Analytics)
- **School of Social Sciences** (BA International Relations & Diplomacy, MA Conflict Resolution & Peacebuilding)

### 3. 🌄 Immersive Parallax Transition
- **"Experience UKH"**: A full-width, smooth parallax scrolling section situated in the center of the homepage between Academic Offerings and Research.
- High-resolution campus photography with clean typography and direct pathways into campus life.

### 4. 🧭 Complete Academic Portal
- **Programmes Directory (`programmes.html`)**: Filterable degree catalog across Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and PhD levels.
- **Programme Detail (`programme-detail.html`)**: Deep dive into individual degrees with integrated interactive AI syllabus explorer.
- **Admissions (`admissions.html`)**: Multi-step application wizard, entry requirements, and deadlines.
- **Research (`research.html`)**: Kurdish Language Models (KurdishGPT), High-Performance Computing GPU cluster, environmental aquifer research.
- **Campus Life (`student-life.html`)**: Student accommodation, athletics, clubs, and student services in Erbil.
- **Floating "Back to Top" Button**: Smooth scroll navigation on all pages.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5 & CSS3**: Pure Semantic HTML5 and Vanilla CSS with custom modern design system (Royal Blue `#1c5496`, Sunburst Cyan `#3ea6e6`, Gold `#d4af37`, and Charcoal Navy).
- **Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)**: Zero external heavy libraries, fast native execution, accessible modals, tabs, and client-side knowledge engine.
- **Responsive & Dark/Light Mode**: Full responsive layout with integrated light and dark theme switching.

---

## 🚀 Running Locally

You can serve this prototype using any local static web server:

```bash
# Using Python 3
python -m http.server 3000

# Or using Node.js npx
npx serve .
```

Open your browser and navigate to `http://localhost:3000`.
