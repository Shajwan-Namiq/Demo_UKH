Application Process

Follow our clear 5-step pathway from choosing your course to receiving your official unconditional offer.

01

Choose your programme

Explore our 25+ degrees and find the specialization matching your ambitions.

02

Check requirements

Review high school or undergraduate GPA marks and language thresholds.

03

Prepare documents

Assemble certified transcripts, passport copy, English test results, and statement.

04

Submit application

Complete our online application portal and receive your reference number.

05

Receive your decision

Receive your formal offer letter and orientation invitation from the Admissions Office.

Required Application Documents

  • Original High School Certificate / Baccalaureate or Bachelor's Degree Transcript
  • Certified English translation of academic records (if issued in another language)
  • Valid Passport (for international students) or National Identity Card
  • Proof of English Language Proficiency: IELTS Academic (5.5 - 6.5 depending on programme) or UKH Placement Test
  • Statement of Purpose (300-500 words for undergraduate; 500-1,000 words for postgraduate)
  • Two academic or professional recommendation letters (Postgraduate applicants)
